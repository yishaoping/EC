Native/EC target-cycle comparison for eight explicitly specified PARSEC-derived profiles on CPU Golden Gate simulation (frozen EC 8491167d, one BOOM at 200 MHz, four EC checkers at 100 MHz, one application thread, matched FASED settings). Bars show EC/Native cycles between identical ROI instrumentation boundaries and require identical retired-instruction counts, matching outputs, and complete EC package acceptance. Currently 8/8 first-round pairs are accepted. Colors distinguish reduced/changed configurations, the16-trial reduced profile, and captured component replay. These exact-profile results do not estimate complete PARSEC simmedium applications; representativeness and linked-address/cache-state effects are uncalibrated. Repetition counts and cycle ranges are supplied in repeatability.csv. Repeated deterministic simulation measures repeatability under the same configuration, without estimating workload sampling uncertainty.
 The descriptive Geomean of all eight declared profiles is 1.672531x, computed as exp(mean(log(EC/Native))); the full-application Geomean is unavailable.

The supplemental checker acceptance-tail data adds max(0,last BOOM-accepted result cycle minus ROI-end cycle) to EC ROI cycles and divides by Native ROI cycles. This measured endpoint metric omits EC startup/enable costs and does not assert complete SVB or architectural drain, strict detection latency, or rollback latency. It is excluded from the primary ROI Geomean.
